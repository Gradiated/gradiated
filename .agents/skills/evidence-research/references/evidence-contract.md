# Evidence artifact contract

Every durable research artifact should make these fields easy to find:

- question and decision
- claim scope and non-scope
- hypothesis and falsification rule
- model, data, workload, software, hardware, and revision identities when relevant
- source table with URL, title, revision/publication date, retrieval date, and evidence type
- method and exact commands or configuration paths
- result, uncertainty, limitations, and competing explanations
- next action, kill condition, or reason the work is blocked

Use dated filenames for decision briefs. Keep raw source material separate from the synthesis. Preserve hashes for downloaded artifacts when byte identity matters.
