# Model announcements

We'd like to produce some model announcement content. To decide what our target is I have 3 main questions.

1. What happened for the last 8 important model drops? Where can we fit in to the timeline?
2. What's our plan to stay informed?
3. What content would we like to include in our model announcement template?

## 1. What happened for the last 8 important model drops?

[The last 8 model drops](https://claude.ai/artifact/FbvE5G2QsEYFw714C6oA47)

### Thoughts

- Model announcements are often not the day that the weights are published. They usually don't provide an expected weight drop date saying something like "within the month"
- A model is usually announced by a blog, and then an X post
- On model announcement day it's usually available on the provider's API and Open Router very shortly (hours) after.
- Where a date was not specified, labs usually post on X  or blog that the weights have dropped shortly after they're live on Hugging Face.

TLDR: We can publish in the window between a model's public announcement and when the weights are live, but depending on what we wish to say, we can also prepare during that time and publish shortly after the model drop.
