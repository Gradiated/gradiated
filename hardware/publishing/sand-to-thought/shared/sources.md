## Fabrication teaching references

Used for [teaching Lecture 3](../lectures/lecture-3/script.md); checked on 29 October 2026. The downloaded PDFs remain local alongside the other reference decks.

- [Washington University — Fabrication of MOSFETs](https://classes.engineering.wustl.edu/cse463/Chapter_2_CSE463.pdf): PDF page 8 shows a self-aligned transistor sequence; pages 9–10 pair CMOS cross-sections with masks. Page 6 uses negative photoresist; our article and teaching script use positive photoresist.
- [MIT 6.152J, Fall 2005 — Lecture 18: CMOS](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-152j-micro-nano-processing-technology-fall-2005/57460d7d1e3ffe8e245f4f2eb3d8f845_lecture18.pdf): supporting reference for planar transistor fabrication, CMOS inverter structure and LOCOS isolation.
- [NPTEL — CMOS fabrication sequence](https://archive.nptel.ac.in/content/storage2/courses/117101058/Slides/12.7.htm): written explanation of gate formation, self-alignment, contacts and wiring.

- [Branch Education — How are Microchips Made?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dX9CGRZwD-w): the six equipment categories in Aidan’s screenshot and the 80-pattern/940-step illustrative process. The video introduces these through a cake analogy and later distinguishes photomask patterns from physical wiring levels. The relevant wording was checked via a [transcript copy](https://pickscribe.com/v/dX9CGRZwD-w); the figures are examples rather than a universal fabrication specification.
- [TRUMPF — semiconductor manufacturing](https://www.trumpf.com/en_US/solutions/industries/semiconductor-manufacturing/), FAQ “How is a microchip manufactured?”: several hundred, sometimes over a thousand production steps, taking months. Used for the general scale in Lecture 3.
