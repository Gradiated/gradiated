# Inference research

Compare models and implementations across chips using reproducible workloads and explicit quality requirements. Begin with the existing KV260 transformer, then add devices without changing what old measurements mean.

## Current implementation

[KV260 implementation](implementations/kv260/README.md) contains the consolidated Mac reference/model work and Linux circuit/board work. Shared registries below refer to it, rather than duplicating the code or weights.

[Physical results](results/ff-exp-div-20261116T230715Z-0969cc/findings.md) · [Experiment handoff](experiments/kv260-ff-exp-div-handoff.md).

The feed-forward breakdown on the adopted logits-8-lanes-v1 baseline is complete. Simulation replayed seven cases and 716 token positions with all 11 RTL modules unchanged. Exact outputs, cache contents, bus events and phase/cycle records match the archived baseline. Each position takes 34,306 feed-forward cycles: 25,728 (75.0%) in scalar activation/gating send and wait states, 8,256 (24.1%) in the final down projection, 193 in gate/up pair handling and 129 in storage/start control. These are disjoint elapsed controller windows. Child arithmetic overlaps them and must not be added again.

The exponential's three divisions by the fixed denominator 45,426 occupy 12,672 cycles (36.9% of feed-forward time). The sigmoid's separate runtime division occupies another 4,224. Gate and up projections each execute 8,320 arithmetic/read/rounding cycles, but all except 65 overlap scalar processing. Accelerating them alone has less than 0.1% whole-request potential under this unchanged schedule. The next candidate for discussion is an exact specialised divider for the feed-forward exponential, with down-projection READ/MAC overlap as a simpler separate option. These are proposals, not implemented speed-ups. Preserve exact quotient, remainder, rounding and supported input-domain behaviour, not just the operands seen in this profile. No acceleration is selected or authorised by this diagnostic. [Diagnostic findings](results/ff-profile-rtl-20261116T211748Z/findings.md) · [Protocol](experiments/kv260-feed-forward-profile-v1.md). This used the Mac simulator only. No board or Linux-server operation, resource build or new power measurement occurred.

Experiment 7 compared eight lanes directly with the saved four-lane implementation. Matched simulation, the routed 50 MHz build and the independently audited physical comparison passed. Decode rate rose 1.1–1.7%, complete-request time fell 0.7–1.6%, and gross VCC_SOM energy per answer/output token fell 0.66–1.05% across three paired blocks. These are small observed differences on three prompts in one board boot, not a general energy guarantee. The whole circuit adds a net 393 LUTs, 322 registers and four DSPs, with block RAM unchanged. Original circuit and weights are restored and checked. Aidan subsequently adopted logits-8-lanes-v1 as the selected baseline. [Physical findings](results/logits-8-lanes-20261116T202250Z-44df9d/findings.md) · [Completed handoff](experiments/kv260-logits-eight-handoff.md). Ethernet and broader serving benchmarks remain deferred. This was an explicit adoption decision.

Experiment 6 compared one, two and four lanes in the final token-score projection. [Two-lane physical results](results/logits-2-lanes-20261116T164332Z-6c4968/findings.md) show 4.2–6.5% higher decode rate and 3.6–4.3% less gross module energy per answer. [Four-lane results](results/logits-4-lanes-20261116T170727Z-020546/findings.md) show 6.4–10.0% higher decode rate and 5.7–6.1% less energy. ## Local recovery and restoration

The storage register records the original source manifests, the active Mac manifest after interpreter relocation, and their SHA-256 hashes. Use `verify-artifacts` with the appropriate manifest and root before relying on a recovered copy. Keep the manifests with the payloads when copying to another machine. The Linux archive contains build and board evidence, not a Mac-executable environment.

The current Mac environment is self-contained under `.agents/runtime/hardware/inference/`, and the original worktree remains intact. Protect that runtime’s selected model payloads and the recovery archive from generic cache cleanup. The source repository alone does not contain these bytes. A fresh checkout needs a verified artifact restore and a compatible pinned environment before running the model checks. Shared backup and independent-machine restoration remain pending.

The local `gradiated-hardware-wip` checkout links its ignored `models/`, `python/` and `venv/` runtime directories to the verified copies in `/Users/aidanmcconnell/dev/gradiated/.agents/runtime/hardware/inference/`. Selected historical run folders referenced by `model/conditioned.json` are copied and checksum-verified within its output directory, as required by the runtime’s path checks. Its temporary files, cache and new outputs stay in the new checkout. These links are not an additional backup. Keep the original artifact directory in place.
