The selected circuit is **attention-value-pair-v1**, adopted by Aidan after Experiment 23. The frozen model remains **chip-joke-text-20261109 W16A16**, at 50 MHz with a 256-position context. ## Status and progression

- **Completed:** evidence exists for the stated scope. Simulation, physical measurement and baseline adoption are separate facts.
- **Partial:** some evidence exists, with the remaining work stated explicitly.
- **Prepared:** a protocol or harness exists, but the named suite has not run. Preparation is not a result.
- **Proposed:** an idea awaiting selection and a bounded comparison plan.
- **Deferred:** intentionally postponed until a stated need, model or instrument makes it useful. A deferred suite may already be prepared.

A promising idea can become a sequence of experiments:

1. **Explore:** change one representative component and test a clear hypothesis against an exact baseline.
2. **Confirm:** check that any benefit survives exact correctness, implementation timing and matched measurements. Check whether the difference is large enough to distinguish from observed variation.
3. **Extend:** if worthwhile, propose applying the mechanism to other suitable components, preserving separately named versions and comparing each extension. Similar-looking arrays may have different access patterns, port needs or timing constraints. An extension is not automatically authorised by the exploratory test.
4. **Adopt or retain as an alternative:** record the benefit, resource cost and limitations. Aidan explicitly selects new baselines. A smaller or lower-power alternative may remain useful even when it is not selected.

For example, moving one array from block RAM to distributed RAM tests the mechanism. A useful result can lead to a broader memory implementation, but it does not imply every weight array, working buffer and KV-cache array should move. Stop extending when the benefit disappears, costs outweigh it or a different bottleneck dominates. Preserve neutral and unsuccessful outcomes too.

## Outstanding teaching experiments

Experiment 12 compared block RAM and distributed RAM for a single weight array. Broader changes to memory placement or access are separate proposals. Experiment 14 tested serial reads of the same array from DDR4 on the adopted Experiment 13 circuit. Experiment 15 compared beat reuse and whole-row bursts on a matched fixture. Experiment 16 moved the 20 KiB final-logits operand copy to DDR4 bursts on a separately routed candidate. All 200 timed replies matched the reference and original restoration verified all 320 BOS scores. Median completion changed +5.41% to +11.58% across the three prompts. Gross mixed-block VCC_SOM joules per verified answer changed +8.55%. The routed design used 39 on-chip control BRAM tiles and 31 candidate BRAM tiles, with the input embedding retained on-chip. Experiment 13 remained selected after that comparison. [Physical findings](../results/logits-ddr4-burst-20261119T121954Z-31256a/findings.md) · [Protocol](kv260-logits-ddr4-burst-v1.md)

Experiment 17 overlapped final-score arithmetic with fetches from DDR4 on a matched 50 MHz fixture. Both arms accessed the same DDR4 bytes per position and median completion took 1.26–2.54% less time. Gross mixed-block module energy per verified answer changed -1.87%. [Physical findings](../results/logits-ddr4-prefetch-20261119T151537Z-9eeb65/findings.md) · [Protocol](kv260-logits-ddr4-prefetch-v1.md)

Experiment 18 tested exact shortened attention probability division against the selected on-chip Experiment 13 circuit at 50 MHz. Across three prompts, median completion took 4.80–7.64% less time. Pooled gross mixed-block VCC_SOM joules per verified answer changed -6.46%. The [prior forecast](kv260-attention-context-cost-v1/findings.md) reconciled against all 256 occupied depths and seven complete-request forecasts in the [exact RTL audit](../results/probability-div-v1-rtl-20261119T160135Z/findings.md). The [physical run](../results/probability-div-20261119T161808Z-5b91c1/findings.md) exercised three natural prompts. Aidan adopted `probability-div-v1` after this comparison. [Protocol](kv260-probability-div-v1.md)

The [saved attention profile](../results/attention-profile-rtl-20261117T104256Z/findings.md), with the exact savings from [Experiment 10](../results/attention-cache-pipeline-rtl-20261117T105443Z/findings.md) and [Experiment 11](../results/attention-exp-div-rtl-20261117T131434Z/findings.md), reconciles to the Experiment 11 request totals. On that circuit the exponential phase occupied 9.6–14.7% of complete-request cycles across the three prompts. Ideally halving that whole phase would save 4.8–7.4% before scheduling and memory overhead. These are derived bounds, not measured speed-ups. Two simultaneous exponential table loads need replication or arbitration, and short contexts and early-zero results reduce utilisation. Parallel heads require additional scratch storage and control and are a separate proposal.

## Measurement, history and maintenance

Use [measurement standard v2](measurement-standard-v2.md) and the [comparison template](comparison-template.md) for new comparisons. Preserve v1 and completed protocols unchanged. Core physical comparisons include correctness, workload/output counts, TTFT, decode TPS, completion time, internal cycles, named-boundary active/idle power, J/answer, J/output token, tokens/J, LUT/register/DSP/BRAM counts and percentages, implementation timing and observed variation. Include conditional evidence relevant to the intervention, such as memory stalls or stage-cycle breakdowns. Do not repeat every broad suite after every small change. Reuse unchanged static reports with exact references and state unavailable measurements explicitly.

Keep model, numerical rules, clock and host software matched except for the declared change. Report simulation, analytical predictions and physical results separately. For cross-chip work, report total and per-request throughput and quality under matching conditions. Byte tokens and subword tokens are not interchangeable. User counts require request frequency, think time, workload mix and response targets. Compare equivalent electrical boundaries or label their differences. Fewer cycles alone do not prove lower energy.

For each selected idea, add a linked protocol and version identity. After execution, record the starting baseline, intended change, source/build pins, results, resource/energy trade-offs, limits and adoption decision. Move or update its row rather than leaving completed work described as a future proposal. Keep extensions linked to their parent experiment and retain failures. A completed experiment does not automatically become the baseline.

Protocols live in `experiments/`, workload manifests in `workloads/`, and immutable records in `results/<run-id>/`. Preserve raw evidence and source/build artifacts by hash. This roadmap is a navigation and decision list, not a replacement for those records. Updating it does not run experiments, modify publishing, commit, push or deploy anything.
