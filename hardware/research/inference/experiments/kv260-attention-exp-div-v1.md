## Exact arithmetic and predicted effect

Attention subtracts the largest signed 32-bit score from each score using sign-extended 64-bit operands. The difference is non-positive and at least `−(2³² − 1)`. The generic implementation returns zero below `−1,048,576`. For every remaining input, the first division's magnitude is at most 1,048,576, so its prefix after dropping six bits is at most 16,384. In the second division, dropping eight bits returns the first division's remainder, at most 45,425. In the final division, the numerator is the signed difference between two accepted table entries multiplied by a remainder at most 45,425. The interface caps each table word at `0x80000000`, so the magnitude is at most `2³¹ × 45,425`. This fits a signed 64-bit word and the prefix after dropping 32 bits is at most 22,712.

All three prefixes are smaller than 45,426. The omitted quotient digits are zero and the prefix itself is the remainder. The helper starts the original radix-four recurrence from that prefix and retains the sign, exact remainder and round-to-nearest-even rule. Its generic fallback handles out-of-range prefixes. Cutoff and table faults use the existing paths. Exercise both worst-case signs, the full accepted table range, oversized table-word rejection, missing tables, ties, reset in flight and result backpressure. The direct divider fixture also covers the wider arithmetic bound for arbitrary unsigned 32-bit table words.

The three divisions decrease from 96 to 23 steps per non-cutoff exponential. Applying the saving of 73 steps to the [independent profile](../results/attention-profile-rtl-20261117T104256Z/findings.md) predicts 741,096, 1,323,052 and 2,871,820 fewer complete-request cycles for the three measured prompts. Against the adopted control these are 12.09%, 14.32% and 17.37%. The [seven-case predictions](../implementations/kv260/variants/attention-exp-div-v1/predictions.json) specify the prefix, first output, last output and completion boundaries. These are cycle predictions. Latency, energy, timing margin and resource use require separate evidence.
