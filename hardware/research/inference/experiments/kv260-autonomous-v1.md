## Question and causal hypothesis

Does moving prefix sequencing, exact greedy selection, feedback and stopping from the CPU to the FPGA reduce complete-request latency and VCC_SOM energy while preserving every output and all neural calculations?

Existing observations show score-transfer waits in three final-request snapshots and a measured improvement from Python score copying. Those observations justify this intervention but do not predict its speed-up. Competing explanations include arithmetic remaining dominant, host polling/collection replacing other CPU costs, and extra controller power offsetting shorter execution. The modifiable end-to-end fraction is unknown. A correct matched comparison with no material improvement falsifies the performance hypothesis for these workloads, without invalidating the implementation.
