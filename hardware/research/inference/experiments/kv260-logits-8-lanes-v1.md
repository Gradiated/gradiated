# Eight versus four lanes for final token scores

Test whether eight multiply-and-accumulate lanes in the final 320-by-32 token-score projection improve inference on the KV260 relative to the exact four-lane experiment 6 circuit. Keep the frozen `chip-joke-text-20261109` W16A16 model, 50 MHz clock target, tokenisation, numerical rules, host observer, AU02 stream, cache and three-prompt schedule fixed. The selected research baseline is `scalar-div-v1`.

Change only the lane parameter from four to eight. Share one read of each activation across all lanes. Each lane processes one output row in the same arithmetic order as the control, reading its weights from its own bank. The eight banks hold 1,280 weights each and use the same total of 10,240 signed 16-bit weights. Complete each group's eight outputs in ascending row order through the existing serial interface. A group uses one prime clock, 32 MAC clocks, one rounding clock and eight output handshakes. Both arms retain 320 serial output handshakes per full projection.

With no output stalls, the control takes 80 × 38 = 3,040 clocks per projection and the candidate takes 40 × 42 = 1,680. Saving 1,360 clocks per retained scored position is 27.2 µs at 50 MHz. Predicted full request counts are 7,799,105, 11,611,357 and 20,482,387 clocks for the three natural cases in schedule order (Hello, six-byte and 48-byte prompt), with 1.56%, 1.42% and 0.69% smaller internal completion counts. These are predictions. Host-observed timing and energy require measurements. Useful weight bytes per projection stay fixed at 20,480, so the analytical rates at 50 MHz are 336.8 and 609.5 MB/s for four and eight lanes.

We expect eight projection DSPs and 37 DSPs overall. One 36-Kibit RAM primitive per 1,280 × 16-bit bank would leave the expected total of 38 block RAM tiles. The actual bank layout, memory count, LUTs, registers, setup and hold come from the routed design. More fanout on the shared activation and a wider output selector increase the timing and logic cost. Any energy change may be small compared with measurement variation. Preserve the raw traces and report the range and timestamp-alignment sensitivity.
