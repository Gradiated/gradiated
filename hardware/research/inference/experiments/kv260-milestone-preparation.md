Run them from the repository root with `python3 -B -m unittest discover -s hardware/research/inference/workloads -p 'test_kv260_milestones.py'`.

Connect them inside the existing unprivileged sensor process with the pinned sensor checks. Verify longer-window storage, the watchdog and unchanged read overhead. These adapters do not connect to the board on their own. Keep current device temperature support and calibration limits visible.
