RoPE: give a pair of numbers a position

Rotary Position Embedding adjusts Q and K according to where a token appears. Think of two numbers as an arrow’s coordinates: RoPE rotates that arrow. Different pairs rotate at different speeds.

Four multiplications run one after another. One pair takes six simulated clock edges after its start; accepting pairs and receiver stalls add clocks. This separate block has its own multiplier. It is not yet wired to the projection circuit.
