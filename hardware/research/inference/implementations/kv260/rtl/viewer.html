Step through the observed simulation

For each output, the circuit reads and accumulates 32 pairs, then rounds once. It clears the total, moves to the next row of weights and reuses the same multiplier. Each row takes 65 clock edges after its start; accepting results and any receiver stalls add clocks. FPGA frequency has not been measured.

What exactly are we looking at?

This is Verilator RTL simulation on a Mac. It checks hardware-description behaviour; it is not an FPGA placement diagram, gate-level synthesis, board measurement or full transformer execution. It calculates the linear Q/K/V projections from inputs already prepared in Python. Positional rotation of Q/K, the cache and attention are later work.

The multiplier and memories will be mapped to physical FPGA resources in a later build. A gate-level explanation of arithmetic is a separate teaching view.
