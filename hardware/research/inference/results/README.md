The feed-forward breakdown on the adopted logits-8-lanes-v1 baseline is complete. Simulation replayed seven cases and 716 token positions with all 11 RTL modules unchanged. Exact outputs, cache contents, bus events and phase/cycle records match the archived baseline. Each position takes 34,306 feed-forward cycles: 25,728 (75.0%) in scalar activation/gating send and wait states, 8,256 (24.1%) in the final down projection, 193 in gate/up pair handling and 129 in storage/start control. These are disjoint elapsed controller windows. Child arithmetic overlaps them and must not be added again. The exponential's three divisions by the fixed denominator 45,426 occupy 12,672 cycles (36.9% of feed-forward time). The sigmoid's separate runtime division occupies another 4,224. Gate and up projections each execute 8,320 arithmetic/read/rounding cycles, but all except 65 overlap scalar processing. Accelerating them alone has less than 0.1% whole-request potential under this unchanged schedule. The next candidate for discussion is an exact specialised divider for the feed-forward exponential, with down-projection READ/MAC overlap as a simpler separate option. These are proposals, not implemented speed-ups. Preserve exact quotient, remainder, rounding and supported input-domain behaviour, not just the operands seen in this profile. No acceleration is selected or authorised by this diagnostic. [Simulation findings](ff-profile-rtl-20261116T211748Z/findings.md) · [Independent audit](ff-profile-rtl-20261116T211748Z/audit-manifest.json). No new board speed, power, resources or timing margin were measured.
