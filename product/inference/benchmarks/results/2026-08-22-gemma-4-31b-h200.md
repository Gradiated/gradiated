## Capacity and cost conclusion

For the shorter workload, concurrency 12 is the latency-oriented operating
point and 16 is the throughput ceiling. Requests above 16 should spill to
another node or be rejected; concurrency 24 reduced throughput and sharply
increased tail TTFT.

The Nebius account calculator returned $4.50/hour and $3,285 per 730-hour month
for the VM preset, excluding storage and AWS/VPN costs. At the sustained
short-workload peak this is $2.052 per million output tokens or $0.476 per
million total processed tokens. At production prices of $0.12/M input and
$0.35/M output, fully utilized revenue is $1.639/hour versus $4.50/hour compute,
a $2.861/hour or $2,089/month compute loss. Break-even requires approximately
2.75 times the current input and output prices before ancillary costs.
