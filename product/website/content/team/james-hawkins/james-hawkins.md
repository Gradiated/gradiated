## Bio

I started off as a bad professional cyclist, became a worse developer, bootstrapped an online marketing company, wound up as a VP Sales at an enterprise software company, then got into YC where PostHog was born after five pivots, we scaled to become a unicorn, got past $100M in annual revenue and continue to grow fast, simple really! I wanted to create Gradiated because I felt it was a realistic shot to grow 10-100x faster because AI is changing the world and we could use our leverage as a successful company to play a central role in that for many people.

I'm very lucky that work to me feels like play. That said, when I'm not working on Gradiated, I'm spending time with our children, at the gym or gravel biking.

I am in the office several days a week, and usually once or twice a week I'll work very late at night UK time, until 11.30pm or midnight depending how I feel. I try very hard to avoid weekend work. I travel to the US frequently - usually four ish times a year.

## Quirks

- I speak really fast and hop around a lot. I've tried to fix this before but it led to me focusing more on polish than the topic at hand - a tradeoff that I felt wasn't worth it. If you leave a conversation without clarity with me, get me in written form instead.
- I'm a little disorganized. I compensate for this by making sure the teams I work on have this skill. Often I think this actually helps me prioritize the things that really matter. I very rarely get constructive feedback here now, and Scott and AI are both helping a lot.
- I'm an optimist and I'm level headed. If you've got bad news, I am never going to get mad.
- I'm not great at delivering direct feedback live, but I'm good at this if you ask me to do it async or if I have some preparation time.

## What I value

- Proactivity. Do not ask me for permission to do things - I wouldn't have hired you if I didn't trust you. I'd rather 9 things get done well and 1 thing I disagree with than we don't get anything done at all.
- Ambition. Yes, and. People being open minded to very large, scary-at-first ideas. Everything you've ever been impressed by in other businesses was just the product of a bunch of people. There is no reason we can't do it too.
- People who get things done without me having to chase. I love it if we mention something should get done and there's a PR for it the next day.
- People who solve frustrations they have with other people (or with projects / tasks) directly. I won't mediate your relationship with someone else - I'll ask you to speak to them directly.
- Iteration over analysis. Analysis is valuable but it should be _very_ quick and intense.
- Speed. It's much easier to get things right if you take more shots at goal. Let's just get something up and iterate on it.
- Directness impresses me. If you don't like something please just say so. It makes for much healthier relationships.
- Being different / independent thought. It's more effective to be polarizing than boring.

## How I can help you
