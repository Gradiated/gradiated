Our mission is to make it practical for anyone to work with intelligence they can run, adapt, and afford.

As software takes on more of the work people do, the models it depends on will matter as much as the software itself. People should be able to own, fork, and modify those models, choose where they run, and keep using them if a provider changes its prices or terms.

We want a future where millions of people and companies build and improve models for their own work. Distributing that ability gives more people a say in what intelligence is used for and who benefits from it. The freedom to build should not depend on the continuing permission of a handful of companies.

Open weights only give people a real choice if the model can do the job. Our work is to make open models useful enough that people choose them for the work they need done, without lowering their standards.

We ship tools to test and improve models around real work, and release models that others can run and build on. As demand grows, we make the systems underneath them more efficient, so more useful work can get done with less compute and energy.

We open source the vast majority of our work. We make money when people use our cloud products, and design our tools so people can also use them locally on infrastructure they control.

We’ll know we’re making progress when more people can put open models to work, adapt them to their needs, and build things they remain free to run.
