Welcome to the team!

We care _a lot_ about getting your first few weeks right. We want you to feel confident that you've chosen the right place - to work, to feel at home quickly, and to have plenty to get excited about as we build what comes next. By the end of your first month, you should feel settled in and understand where you can make a big contribution.

We mostly work [in person](/handbook/in-person/), and onboarding is where that pays off most. Everyone starts with a week in Cambridge (UK), including people joining remote roles, so everyone gets a similar start. If an existing commitment makes that first week tricky, we'll arrange another week within your first month.

## Before your first day

Getting set up should feel frictionless, so we start as soon as your contract is signed. By the time you arrive:

- Your laptop has been shipped to you.
- Your Google, Slack, GitHub, and Brex accounts are set up.
- Most of your security setup and permissions are ready.
- If your role is remote, your travel to Cambridge for onboarding is booked.

Once the paperwork is done, we move from email to Slack for everything else.

## Your week in Cambridge

Make the most of your time together. There's no fixed timetable: you'll pick up context from the more structured sessions and the casual chats over coffee and dinner. The week usually includes:

- An introduction to our values and strategy
- A session with your interviewers on the feedback from your interviews and SuperDay
- A deep dive into the code you'll work on first
- A "no stupid questions" session, where you bring everything you've wondered about since starting
- A conversation with <Person name="James Hawkins" /> or <Person name="Josh Snyder" />, or both, to agree what good looks like at 30, 60, and 90 days and beyond
- Finishing the setup of your equipment and accounts
- Time with each person on the team, so you know who does what
- Shipping something: aim for a real change merged to main by the end of the week, however small
- Time away from work, like dinner or exploring Cambridge as a group

If you're traveling in for the week, book and pay for it with your Brex card, out of your $5,000 monthly limit. The week should cost a lot less than that, but if you need more room, request a higher limit in Brex. Keep it sensible, and use your best judgment, as with any [spending](/handbook/spending-company-money/). A team favourite is [Wilde Hotel](https://maps.app.goo.gl/gaWJgne3QvNvJUrv7) but you're welcome to book wherever you feel most comfortable.

## 30, 60, and 90-day check-ins

Your manager checks in with you at 30, 60, and 90 days to make sure feedback is flowing both ways through your first three months.

- At 30 days, you'll talk about how things are going, what's working, and anything to change.
- At 60 days, if things are on track, you'll hear it plainly, so you're not left wondering.
- At 90 days, you'll have a clear idea of whether you're on track. If your [probation](/handbook/compensation/) is three months, this is where you'll hear that you've passed. It should never be a surprise, because the earlier check-ins will already have told you where you stand.

In research, hardware, and software optimization roles, probation lasts six months. After your 90-day check-in, you'll keep having monthly check-ins with your manager.
