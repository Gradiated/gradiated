We offer our team unlimited time off, but with an expectation that you take _at least 25 days off a year_, including national holidays. This is to make sure that people can take time off flexibly while not feeling guilty about being on vacation.

The reason for this policy is that it's critical for Gradiated that we hire people we can trust to be responsible with their time off - enough that they can recharge, but not so much that it means we don't get any work done. We'll look into holiday usage occasionally to encourage people who haven't taken the minimum time off to do so. The 25 days is a minimum, not a guide.

As general guidance, we don't care about a few days here and there. If you are taking significantly more vacation time than most - for example, 40 days - we would be very surprised if you aren't causing a strain on the rest of your team as a result. Especially when we're very early on as a project and don't have a lot of people working here.

## Permissionless time off

We care about your results, not how long you work.

You do not need to get approval for time off from your manager. Instead, we expect everyone to coordinate with their team to make sure that we're still able to move forward in your absence. You should avoid things like:

- Having an entire team off at once - this means we can't support customers or keep work moving
- Having the only people who can do some totally critical task at Gradiated off - if this is unavoidable, try to make sure one of you can at least check in if something goes horribly wrong

### How to book time off in Gradiated People

We book time off in <PrivateLink href="https://app.windmill.dev/a/gradiated/platform">Gradiated People</PrivateLink>. Sign in with your Gradiated account.

Before you start, make sure that you have subscribed to the <PrivateLink href="https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?src=c_7ca3a00714164164d9fe7396ccfe0b78c58c796ad3a60bf947d4c02c833fefe7%40group.calendar.google.com&ctz=Europe%2FLondon">team time off calendar</PrivateLink>. Everyone's booked time off shows up there, so it's the easiest way to see who's out before you plan around them.

To book a day off:

- Book it in Gradiated People. It will be approved automatically and added to the team time off calendar (with the exception of longer-term leave).
- There are four types of time off you can select:
  - PTO - this will be the majority of the time, for vacation or any other time off that doesn't fit below
  - [Out sick](#you-are-sick) - this is when you're sick and will take time off unexpectedly
  - [Parental leave](#parental-leave)
  - [Medical leave](#you-are-sick) - this is planned time off for you personally, on medical grounds. If it is a family member who requires support, meaning you will be off, this is PTO.
- Mark the dates in your calendar using Google Calendar's [out of office feature](https://support.google.com/calendar/answer/7638168) - this auto-declines any meetings you get invited to while you're away.
- Set an out of office message on your email and have it point to someone else on the team.
- Set your Slack status to show that you're out and the date you'll be back. This doesn't happen automatically, so please do it before you go.

<Callout type="action" title="Add your own bank holidays">
  Please book in any public holidays you plan to take off as well - we don't add
  them for you. Most of the team is in the UK, and it's common for people based
  there to take UK bank holidays off, but some people prefer to work on certain
  days even if they're a public holiday where they live or are visiting.
</Callout>

The same rules as above apply regardless of the holiday length and type. Sick leave and any other types of time off should also be booked in the same way.

### How to cancel time off

If you decide to cancel your holiday, request the change in Gradiated People.

## Flexible working

We operate on a trust basis and we don't count hours or days worked. We trust everyone to manage their own time.

Whether you have an appointment with your doctor, school run with your kids, or you want to finish an hour early to meet friends or family - we don't mind and you don't need to tell us. Please just add it to your calendar and, if you are doing anything that could require you to be immediately available (for example, a customer-facing role), please make sure you have cover.

## When you should have time off

### You are sick

If you are sick, you don't need to work and you will be paid - the upper limit for paid sick leave for your country will be specified in your contract. This is assuming you need a day or two off, then just take them.

Please let your manager know if you need to take time off due to illness as soon as you are able to, and add it to Gradiated People. You shouldn't preemptively book a bunch of days off sick, as you can't know how long you will actually be sick for and you may trigger the need for a doctor's note (see below). Just book the day or two off that you are sick, then add more if you still feel unwell.

For extended periods of illness (five or more work days), or if you are going over the limit in your country or state, please speak to <Person name="Scott Lewis" /> so we can work out a plan. In most countries, we will need a doctor's note from you.

If you have a medical condition you know will take you away from work regularly, please let Scott know so we can work out accommodations with you and your manager.

### Bereavements and child loss

We do not define "closeness" and we won't ask about your relationship to the person or what they meant to you. Please just let us know up front how much time you would like to take.

Our bereavement policy also covers pregnancy and child loss for both parents, with no questions asked. Please take at least two weeks of paid leave.

If you need extended time for physical or mental health reasons, we will treat it as extended sick leave - just chat to Scott.

### Jury duty, voting, and childcare disasters, aka life stuff

There are lots of situations where life needs to come first. Please let it - just be communicative with your team and fit your work around it as you need. We trust you will do the right thing here.

If you are summoned for jury duty, please let Scott know right away - we can often get an exception granted if we have enough notice.

## Parental leave

Parental leave is exceptional as it needs to be significantly longer than a typical vacation. Anyone at Gradiated, regardless of gender, is able to take parental leave, and regardless of whether you've become a parent through childbirth or adoption.

This table explains the amount of paid time off, depending on how long you've been at Gradiated.

| Time at Gradiated | Maternity leave | Paternity leave |
| ----------------- | --------------- | --------------- |
| Under 6 months    | 3 weeks         | 2 to 3 weeks    |
| 6 to 12 months    | 12 weeks        | 2 to 3 weeks    |
| Over 12 months    | Up to 24 weeks  | 6 weeks         |

<Callout type="fyi" title="How this works with statutory leave">
  Parental leave at Gradiated is designed to be more generous than your local
  jurisdiction's legal requirements. This means that in most cases you will
  receive the Gradiated policy; if you live in a country with more generous
  parental leave, then you will receive that. This policy is not designed to be
  _in addition_ to your specific state or country policy. We use the "top up"
  method to administer paid leave: if you receive any statutory leave, we will
  top it up to your full pay.
</Callout>

We only pay the enhanced parental leave in one continuous block.

Parental leave isn't supposed to be combined with our unlimited PTO policy here - we aren't prescriptive and will trust your judgement. If you need a longer break after childbirth or a staggered return, log it in Gradiated People or speak to your manager. But please note that we usually won't allow you to combine parental leave with a long holiday on top to extend your time off.

Please log parental leave in Gradiated People as soon as you feel comfortable doing so, and in any case at least four months before it will begin. Scott will follow up on any logistical arrangements around salary and any statutory paperwork that needs doing.

### Maternity leave

The above is in reference to paid time off. Maternity leave can be extended using unpaid time off. Please work with your team to find a reasonable solution for both your family and your team, and log the total amount of time you expect to take off in Gradiated People as soon as possible.

For quota-carrying sales roles taking 12 weeks or longer, your OTE will be calculated by averaging your sales quota attainment for the prior two full quarters (capped at 100% OTE).

### Paternity leave

We do not offer unpaid leave for paternity leave.
