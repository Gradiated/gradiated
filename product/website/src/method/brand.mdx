Everything is considered.

Every word, line, colour, and interaction should have a reason to exist. The living reference is [the style page](/style/) — components, tokens, and copyable values. This page records the reasoning behind it; where the two disagree, the style page is right.

## Principles

### Begin with the claim

Lead with the thing that is true or useful. Context follows. Do not hide a precise idea behind scene-setting or slogans.

### Go down the stack

Show the reasoning beneath the result. The visual system should suggest depth, construction, revision, and relationships between layers — all the way down to the silicon.

### Remove what cannot answer for itself

Decoration needs a purpose. If a word, rule, image, or interaction does not improve meaning or orientation, remove it.

### Prefer evidence to assertion

Specifics are more persuasive than superlatives. Use examples, constraints, measurements, and trade-offs wherever possible.

## The metaphor

The language is silicon. The page is a substrate; sections are dies placed on it; hairlines are the traces between them. Diagrams are drawn the way an engineer draws — one pen, exact widths, mono captions — because the product's argument is engineering.

## Colour

Colour lives in three layers, and the order matters. The literals — the ramps you can copy from the style page — are written in one place only. Above them sit functional families for universal meanings. Above both sit the roles (`--color-substrate`, `--color-text`, `--color-panel`, …), which are what components actually reference and what themes rebind. A component never names a literal.

### The families

**Taupe** is the ground: warm, papery, unhurried — the substrate everything sits on. **Kiln** is ember and gold, the expressive accent. **Grove** is the deep green of considered growth. **Aegean** is a working blue. **Plum** takes the occasional editorial moment. Two neutral runs — light and dark — carry text, panels, and rules.

### Themes

Two ship: **Taupe**, the default, and **Dark**. A theme is one construct — `data-theme` on the root themes the page; on a section it themes that section. A reader's choice is stored and respected.

### Gradients

Two-stop pairs declared with their angle — Terrace, Ember, Tide, Shallows — one bound per theme. On the homepage, colour gradients are rationed deliberately: the hero's beam carries one, and everything else speaks in ink.

## The drawn line

Diagrams and technical surfaces share one pen: the page's ink at 30%, one device pixel wide. Coincident lines are drawn once. Solid ink is reserved for data — bits, values, marks — so that what the system computes always reads darker than what merely frames it.

## Typography

**Instrument Sans** carries titles: geometric structure with open letterforms, presence without heaviness. **Barlow** carries body, navigation, and interface: a slightly narrow, low-contrast grotesk balancing warmth and clarity. **JetBrains Mono** carries code and captions: tall x-height, consistent widths, precise alignment. Large type may be quiet; small type must be exact. Avoid unnecessary weights, ornamental italics, and artificial hierarchy.

## Voice

Write plainly, specifically, and with intellectual confidence. Ask a question only when the product or the next sentence can help answer it. Avoid grand claims, vague philosophy, inflated metaphors, and language that sounds profound without saying anything.

## Motion

Motion reveals structure or state; it never exists to demonstrate cleverness. Scroll-driven scenes are scrubbed, damped, and reversible — the reader owns the clock, and scrolling back plays the scene backwards. Entrances share one gesture: rise a little, sharpen from blur, settle. Ambient life runs on the wall clock, quietly, so a page at rest is never dead.
