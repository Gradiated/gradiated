Gradiated is lean. Spending less gives us more time to make the company work, but saving money at the cost of people being productive is a false economy.

Our policy is based on context rather than pre-approval. Before spending company money, ask:

- Can I explain why this is in Gradiated's best interest?
- Is the cost proportionate to the benefit?
- Would I be comfortable explaining the expense to the whole company?

If the answers are clear, make the purchase. If they are not, ask James or Josh for context before spending.

## What you can spend

Each person has a **$5,000 monthly User Limit in Brex**. Use it for individual software, coworking or collaboration, ordinary equipment, training, and other routine expenses that help you do your job. Ask for a limit increase in Brex if a justified purchase will take you over it.

The limit is a ceiling, not an allowance or a target. Only spend what you can justify under the test above.

Use the relevant company-wide budget for company software, offsites, onboarding, and other centrally managed costs. Laptops and Apple Studio Displays are bought centrally through People & Ops rather than from your User Limit.

## Paying and documenting

Use Brex for work expenses. UK employees should use Revolut when an expense is over **£75 and includes UK VAT**; otherwise use Brex. Ask for the invoice to be addressed to Hiberly Ltd at its registered address so the company can reclaim VAT.

For every expense over **$75 or £75**, attach an itemized receipt and complete the memo within **14 days**. A card slip or cropped image showing only the total is not enough. The record needs to show what was bought.

A useful memo says:

- **What:** the item or service
- **Why:** how it helped the company or your work
- **Context:** the project, event, or people involved

Do not use a personal card for planned company spending. If you do so accidentally, submit a reimbursement in Brex within **90 days** with the itemized receipt and an explanation of why the company card was not used. If you accidentally put a personal purchase on a company card, mark it as personal and repay it immediately.

## How expenses are reviewed

Company expenses are visible internally. Finance reviews every Brex expense over **$1,000**, a sample of expenses below that amount, and every Revolut expense. Team leads receive a monthly report for their direct reports so they can spot patterns with the context Finance may not have.

This is not intended to produce line-by-line permission seeking. It makes a trust-based policy auditable. Missing receipts and vague memos create avoidable work and, if repeated, will be treated as a performance issue.

## Contracts and recurring costs

The person with the most context owns a vendor's spend. That means monitoring usage against the contract, reviewing it regularly, and acting before an overage becomes a recurring surprise.

Tell your team lead and the responsible executive early when a contract is expensive, usage-based, likely to exceed its allowance, or difficult to unwind. Bring Finance in as soon as the financial effect looks material. New contracts or changed legal terms also need Legal; a straightforward renewal on unchanged terms usually does not.

A renewal is not automatic. Check whether we still use the product, can reduce the commitment, or should build or buy something else. The larger and longer the commitment, the earlier other people should have context.

## Defaults for common expenses

- **Equipment:** buy what you need for productive work and a sensible useful life. Prefer good-value or refurbished equipment where it does the job. Centrally issued laptops and displays remain company property and must be returned when you leave.
- **Software:** individual tools are a matter of judgement. Shared software has a higher bar because it creates another place for work, communication, and company data to live. If an individual tool becomes widely used, move it to a company account with a named owner.
- **Travel:** travel economy by default. Pay a little more for a refundable fare when plans are genuinely uncertain. Premium economy can be reasonable for frequent or long-haul travel when someone needs to work the next day; business class is not a company expense. The company does not pay for personal days or personal costs added to a work trip.

## What is not appropriate

Do not expense something whose main benefit is personal, including personal entertainment, groceries, or leisure during business travel. For an expense with both work and personal benefit, charge the company only for the reasonable business portion.

An honest misunderstanding will be handled with clarification. Knowingly spending company money for personal benefit, hiding the nature of an expense, or working around these rules is serious misconduct.
