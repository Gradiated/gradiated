Gradiated is built to stand on its own, while sharing the parts of PostHog that make both companies stronger.

## What we share

We share a cap table and finance. This gives us more flexibility, removes a lot of administrative overhead, and lets the value created by both companies compound together over time. The long-term model is closer to one company absorbing and building on adjacent bets than a portfolio of disconnected businesses.

We share corporate security, including Fleet on company laptops and our Google Workspace. These are company-wide systems where one strong standard is safer and simpler than maintaining two versions.

We also share some distribution. Gradiated can reach PostHog's newsletter and YouTube audiences, while dedicated Gradiated writers build its own voice and body of work.

## What stays separate

We do not share infrastructure. Gradiated's infrastructure is the product, so it would not use the same setup as PostHog. Trying to share it would add communication and context overhead without creating meaningful leverage.

We do not share recruitment. Gradiated must not hire people away from PostHog: doing so would slow PostHog's growth and defeat the purpose of creating a separate company. PostHog should keep compounding, and Gradiated should prove that it can stand on its own two feet.

The principle is simple: share the foundations that compound across both companies, and keep the work that creates each company's advantage independent.
