# Introducing Auto-file: your paperwork, sorted before you open it

Small teams lose about three hours a week hunting for invoices, contracts and receipts that someone saved "somewhere". Acme's new Auto-file ends that.

## How it works
1. **Forward or drop anything.** Email attachments to your team's Acme inbox, drag files onto the Acme window, or snap a photo of a paper receipt with the Acme phone app.
2. **Acme reads it.** Auto-file recognises the document type (invoice, receipt, contract, payslip, insurance letter), the sender, the amount and any due date.
3. **It files itself.** The document lands in the right folder — for example *Suppliers › Northwind Coffee › 2026* — named the way your team names things: `2026-11-03 Northwind invoice 1042 €384.00.pdf`.
4. **Reminders appear on their own.** An invoice due on 20 November gets a reminder three days before, assigned to whoever pays that supplier.

## What teams see in the first week
- 94% of documents filed correctly with no edits (Acme beta, 212 teams).
- Average search time down from 4 minutes to 9 seconds.
- Missed payment deadlines down 71%.

## Stays out of your way
Every filing decision shows up in the Activity feed with an Undo button. You can teach Auto-file a rule in plain words — "anything from Lumen Insurance goes to Insurance › Car" — and it follows it from then on.

## Availability
Auto-file is available today on every Acme plan, at no extra cost. Turn it on under Settings › Auto-file.
