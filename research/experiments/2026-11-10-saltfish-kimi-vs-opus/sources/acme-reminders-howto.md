# How to set up payment reminders in Acme

Acme can remind the right person before every bill is due. Setup takes about two minutes.

## Step 1 — Open Reminders
In the left sidebar, click **Reminders**. If you have never used it, you'll see an empty list and a button that says **New reminder rule**.

## Step 2 — Choose what to remind about
Click **New reminder rule**. Under *Remind me about*, pick **Invoices with a due date**. You can narrow it: for example only suppliers in the *Suppliers* folder, or only amounts over €500.

## Step 3 — Choose when
Under *When*, choose how many days before the due date. Most teams pick **3 days before**. Add a second reminder **on the due date** if you want a safety net.

## Step 4 — Choose who
Under *Who*, pick **Whoever pays this supplier** (Acme learns this from past payments) or name a person, such as *Priya (Finance)*.

## Step 5 — Save and check
Click **Save rule**. Acme immediately shows the next five reminders the rule will send, e.g. *Northwind invoice 1042 — €384.00 — reminder on 17 Nov to Priya*. Click any of them to snooze or reassign it.

## Tips
- Reminders arrive by email and in the Acme phone app.
- Mark an invoice **Paid** and its reminders stop on their own.
- Rules apply to documents already in Acme, not just new ones.
