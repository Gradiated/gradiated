# Experiment records

Keep publishable summaries and aggregate evidence for future posts here.
Use `YYYY-MM-DD-short-name/` with the experiment's start date, a short
`README.md`, and supporting aggregate `data/` and `figures/`.

Keep conversation exports, raw tool activity, and restricted evidence in
approved private storage. Record coverage and redactions alongside any
private export. Do not maintain an experiment index in this README.
