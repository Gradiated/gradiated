## Question

For the exact 4,214-input/358-output workload, what completed-request throughput and concurrency envelope must a one-L40S FP8 server sustain to beat the measured `$0.000614–$0.000621` retail request cost after utilization and operating headroom, and does the current `max_num_seqs=8` contract fit the conservative exact-model memory bound?

The paid owner is retrying only a deterministic hostname defect and still owns all provider mutation. 1. Sweep explicit service utilization, replica/headroom, non-GPU overhead, failure/reload, and storage/orchestration factors; do not use the experimental preemptible rate as production economics.

4. Keeps completion time usable: report p50/p95 completion-time ceilings as explicit sensitivities, not inherited interactive SLOs. Do not turn latency into a hard gate unless reliability or completion time becomes commercially unusable.

Produce `SATURATION_GATE.json`, a small reproducible calculation script, and `DECISION.md`. Label all inputs as measured, derived, or assumed. This is a decision bound, not endpoint evidence and not a substitute for complete GPU requests.
