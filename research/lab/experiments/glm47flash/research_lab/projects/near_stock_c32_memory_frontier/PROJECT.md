## Why it matters

The measured full-model c16 point is 535.286 output tok/s at 98.36% mean GPU utilization and 44,103 MiB peak VRAM. FP8 KV is especially high-information because c32 FP8 raw KV bytes approximately equal c16 BF16 KV bytes; the previous FP8 project never started an FP8 arm and is operationally null, not negative scientific evidence.
