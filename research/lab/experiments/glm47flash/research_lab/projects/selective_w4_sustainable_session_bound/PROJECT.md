# Selective-W4 sustainable session bound

## Question

Does the measured combined selective-W4 c32 candidate have any conservative long-session operating envelope below the campaign's authoritative `$0.514/M` output-token target after setup amortization and explicit omitted-cost reserve, or is its `$0.484858/M` planning-GPU result too narrow to justify later quality recovery?

## Why it matters

Combined head+shared W4 is the strongest exact-full-47 representation result at `630.195398` output tok/s with `2,323 MiB` observed headroom. At the fresh `$1.10/hour` L40S planning rate it leaves only about `5.7%` raw margin to the target, before setup, interruption, retry, storage, transfer, orchestration, or cleanup. Scope localization may identify a quality-plausible isolated candidate, but the portfolio should know in parallel whether the measured mechanism can ever sustain the economic target. The analogous top-3/shared frontier is not transferable because throughput and margin differ; reuse its method, not its conclusion.

## Scope

Work offline at `$0` for at most 600 seconds, 4 GiB RSS, and 32 MiB of new artifacts. Verify the immutable W4 archive and typed summary. Derive the candidate's planning-rate steady-state COGS directly from `630.195398` tok/s, then construct a session-length frontier at fresh production rates `$1.00/$1.10/$1.25` per hour and explicit target reserves `1%/2%/3%/5%`. Separate measured allocation/setup/transfer/load/science/cleanup durations from assumptions about production recurrence. For each cell report required productive seconds/hours, productive fraction, fixed-loss allowance, and whether the measured `1,310.638 s` allocation / `159.965 s` useful-GPU geometry is compatible. Compare the result with the completed top-3 frontier without transferring its workload or quality claims.

Do not run or modify a model, materialize a checkpoint, mutate a provider, search prices, build a launcher, change quality policy, or prepare a successor experiment. The shared price inventory is fresh at `1.323` days; use it as given. Label raw planning-GPU, measured experimental lifecycle, derived session frontier, and unbound production terms separately.

## Decisions

- `W4_SUSTAINABLE_ENVELOPE_ADMISSION`: existing evidence supports a named base-rate session/reliability envelope with nonzero reserve and defensible omitted-cost bound.
- `W4_ENVELOPE_EVIDENCE_OPEN`: mathematical cells exist, but one named production session/reliability or omitted-cost fact remains unbound.
- `W4_MARKET_ONLY_CLOSE`: no conservative cell survives the base planning case and relevant reserves.

Admission does not establish quality eligibility or authorize spend. An evidence-open result must name exactly one missing fact and must not open another project. A market-only close means scope-localization may finish as scientific attribution, but no later W4 recovery/training spend should follow on L40S.
