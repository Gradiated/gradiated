# Model-specific optimization decision register

Updated: 2026-07-12 10:30 CEST

Objective: minimize sustainable input- and output-token cost for a frozen agentic workload while preserving quality, SLOs, availability, and truthful evidence scope.

## Current business denominator

The working commercial gate is 20% below Fireworks GLM-5.2 Standard pricing at 60% gross margin. On the current synthetic cache-heavy agent mix, that permits at most **$0.180976 per million total billable tokens** of infrastructure COGS and requires **2,409.56 billable tokens/s per $1/hour** of fully loaded system cost at the assumed utilization and availability.

Every optimization below must ultimately improve successful, SLO-compliant billable tokens per fully loaded dollar. Storage, bytes, FLOPs, hit rate, or ideal speedup are intermediate mechanisms, not the business result.

## What the experiments currently say

The first pattern is negative but valuable: **single-axis byte reduction does not produce 5–10×**. The quality-passing Qwen selective weight change is capped at 1.327× ideally, and synthetic GLM route placement is capped at 1.551× on fully connected endpoint traffic or 2.641× on ring hop traffic even if the network were the entire runtime.

The explicit NEON result adds an important qualifier: **implementation specialization can remove a large component-level decode tax that byte arithmetic alone misses**. The same packed representation moves from slower than scalar f32 to 3.284× faster than scalar f32 when the inner path is architecture-specific. The fair next denominator is a tuned BF16/Accelerate or production-runtime kernel and then full-model goodput; the current component ratio is not a token-cost multiplier.

The second pattern supports a narrower co-design thesis: **discontinuities matter more than smooth percentage savings**. Expert-selective checkpoint formats can remove devices from the residency floor. Cache policy can trade recompute for movement. Placement can remove multi-hop traffic without adding memory. MTP can remove synchronization rounds only when acceptance repays draft and verification work. These interactions are model-specific and can cross hardware/serving thresholds.

The third pattern is a warning for the business: the strongest apparent number, 5.38×, is invalid as a performance claim because it comes from the least complete workload ledger and is dominated by fixed and coordination assumptions. The [`hardware feasibility audit`](CUSTOM_HARDWARE_FEASIBILITY.md) shows that replacing only throughput totals yields 1.054× on the serial fixture, while replacing only fixed and coordination totals yields 4.079×. Replacing the mechanism fixture with the new whole-decode upper-bound ledger lowers the nominal ratio to 3.592× and shifts its cause to routed-expert HBM traffic under an outdated 3.35 TB/s baseline. A stricter throughput-only counterfactual holds every uncalibrated fixed/control term equal: the custom headline rates are 2.143× over published-peak H200, but preliminary Rubin headline rates are 1.535× faster than the custom profile on the same work. None of those ratios is calibrated end-to-end performance. They do show that the nominal custom rates are not a launch-date moat, so the evidence supports continued software/model co-design and explicitly does not support tapeout.

The dominant uncertainty is now the model/work contract, not the hardware profile. Lower-work versus upper-work output capacity differs by 17.98× on H200, 20.56× on the nominal custom profile, and 24.66× on the Rubin counterfactual. Those ranges are much larger than any modeled hardware separation. Exact-checkpoint expert locality, attention/DSA work, other non-FFN work, and MTP behavior must narrow before hardware ranking can inform investment.

The fourth pattern is a useful model-specific negative result: **selected-index handoff is too small to matter by itself**. IndexShare removes 73.08% of indexer executions and sparse attention removes up to 99.80% of dense semantic-cache reads at one million tokens, both first-order architecture effects already present in GLM-5.2. In contrast, an impossible zero-HBM selected-index handoff improves the modeled component by at most 0.14%. Custom work should target index scanning, irregular compressed-cache gathers, and fusion—not an isolated 8 KB index-list SRAM feature.

The fifth pattern validates the need for fidelity while withholding performance validation: **a plausible optimization can reverse sign when its traffic geometry is expanded**. Logical sorting and page clustering look strongly positive when one selected token is represented by one 32-byte transaction, yet sorting becomes 59.194% worse and cluster64 becomes 2.709% worse when the same experiment issues a synthetic 1,152-byte BF16 record. The engine prevented a false promotion, while its predictive-validity gate admits only one local component holdout whose effect magnitude misses by 44.7%. Falsification utility is not calibration.

## Promotion order

1. Replace the whole-decode ledger's DSA/cache assumptions with the new exact analytical counts while keeping irregular-gather efficiency bounded.
2. Obtain exact-checkpoint route and MTP-acceptance histograms on a privacy-safe frozen agent trace.
3. Calibrate recompute, cache-transfer, expert collective, and scheduler action timings on real hardware.
4. Compare public NVFP4 on tuned vLLM/SGLang before testing a proprietary representation.
5. Feed only calibrated actions into serving/SLO simulation and the $0.180976/M business gate.
6. Escalate surviving matrix/memory/fabric hypotheses to independent simulators and physical feasibility.

## Claim policy

No row currently establishes lower delivered dollars per token than Fireworks, Baseten, Z.AI, tuned vLLM, SGLang, or TensorRT-LLM. A row becomes a business result only when the exact model and intervention pass the frozen quality contract, the same trace passes SLOs, measured or independently validated timing covers the causal actions, and fully loaded cost includes utilization, failures, networking, hosts, memory, energy, engineering, and NRE.

The executable [`current GLM claim-composition report`](../experiments/glm-5.2/current-cost-claim-composition-v1.json) applies that rule across six interventions and seven current evidence records. It admits zero combined output-cost claims. Component or marginal rows in this table are never multiplicative inputs to $/token.
