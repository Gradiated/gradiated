## Exact H100 interpretation

The 62.55 GB BF16 target leaves roughly 17.45 GB of an 80 GB H100 before runtime reservations. At 10K input, resident KV is derived as 1.154 GiB: 50 sliding layers retain 1,024 tokens and 10 global layers retain all 10K. The dominant GEMMs use 5,376 x 21,504 dimensions (both tile-friendly multiples of 128), while decode has small batch M and streams weights from HBM; 50 MB L2 cannot retain the 62.55 GB model. Therefore MTP and scheduler overlap are the most direct runtime opportunities. This is a hardware-informed prior; only the paid trace can falsify it.
