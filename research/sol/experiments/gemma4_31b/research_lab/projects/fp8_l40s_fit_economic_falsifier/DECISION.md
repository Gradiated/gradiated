# Decision: nominate one direct L40S CUDA falsifier

The exact pinned Gemma 4 31B metadata is 32,682,372,656 parameters and 62,546,177,752 BF16 bytes. The tied 262,144 × 5,376 embedding/output alone is 2,818,572,288 BF16 bytes. The explicit language MLP and attention classes account for 29,396,882,160 parameters; the 1,876,258,352-parameter residual is retained in the conservative bound in [MEMORY_ACCOUNTING.json](MEMORY_ACCOUNTING.json). Online FP8 leaves the tied embedding in BF16 and compresses linear weights to one-byte weights plus runtime scales. The conservative 10K-context estimate is 40.504 GiB including 5 GiB runtime reserve, leaving 4.20 GiB under a 0.92 allocation. This is a derived fit bound, not a CUDA measurement.

Nominate exactly one paid arm: one L40S, pinned `vllm/vllm-openai:gemma4` amd64 image, `--quantization fp8`, `--dtype bfloat16`, `--enforce-eager`, `max_model_len=10000`, then a complete 4,214/358 request and one 10K single-stream request. The first request must be consumed by the raw-output correctness boundary. vLLM's first-party issue tracker currently reports Gemma4 FP8 dynamic-output corruption and a separate FP8_BLOCK scaling bug; therefore this arm is an economic/consumer falsifier, not a production-quality claim.

At the shared committed planning rates, L40S must deliver more than 39.2857% of measured H100 throughput before overhead to win raw GPU cost. Use the actual meter only for experiment spend; add queueing, failures, replicas, orchestration, and quality only after measurement. Do not wait for H100 and do not create a support successor.
