# FP8 L40S fit and economic falsifier

## Question

Can the exact pinned Gemma 4 31B checkpoint use a current vLLM FP8 execution path that fits one 48 GB L40S, and is the resulting direct paid arm capable of beating the measured H100 control on sustainable cost without assuming transferred throughput or quality?

## Why now

The H100 owner remains the only provider-spend lane and is making one bounded operational correction. A disjoint `$0` lane can prepare the next representation/placement intervention while that owner stages 62.55 GB of BF16 weights. This is a larger economic discontinuity than the prior MI300X lead: the fresh production inventory prices L40S at `$1.10/GPU-hour` versus H100 SXM at `$2.80/GPU-hour`, so L40S needs at least 39.29% of matched H100 throughput before non-GPU overhead to lower raw GPU cost. MI300X has no price entry; the completed project's `$2.60` claim was the MI355X rate and is not admissible for an MI300X comparison.

Artificial Analysis currently observes exact-model FP8 service from multiple providers, which is a mechanism lead rather than reproducible self-hosting evidence. The campaign's exact target remains `google/gemma-4-31B-it@b9ea41a2887d8607f594846523f94c6cc75ac8a4`, default non-reasoning and text-only. A dynamic or derived FP8 proxy may measure the cost mechanism, but it is not deployable until quality and feature validation pass.

## Decision

- Nominate one direct paid L40S arm when the exact source/consumer path is bound and steady residency plus conservative runtime/KV headroom fits 48 GB. CUDA compilation/load is part of that paid arm, not another gate.
- If the best exact route provably cannot fit, lacks any executable Ada consumer, or requires non-reconstructable model bytes, close it with the quantified reason and nominate no support successor.
- Promotion after a later paid run requires complete requests and lower matched sustainable cost than H100; representation quality remains a separate mandatory production gate.
