## 2026-07-18 — cache-corrected c16/c32 scheduling comparison

Question: which model-specific scheduling interaction is most likely to reduce complete-request H200 cost by at least 5%, and does it survive the exact 4,214/358 workload economics and queueing target?

Action: ranked the available interactions from the existing SGLang c32 endpoint capture, exact H200 c16/c32 decode-linear/head measurements, exact campaign-mix prefill floor, prefix-cache bound, and economic gate. Implemented and ran a deterministic 100,000-request bulk-service event simulation comparing background c16 and c32 pooling. The calibrated service curve starts from the actual c32 whole-request endpoint and corrects its measured one uncached prompt token/request to the campaign's 3,678.822 fresh tokens/request with the impossible-ideal prefill floor; c16 adds the measured c16-minus-c32 component gap. This is deliberately optimistic for the scheduling candidate.

Evidence: measured endpoint telemetry completed 32/32 exact-token requests in 11.625702 s with mean TTFT 1.758618 s and mean E2E 11.582231 s, but Prometheus deltas prove 134,848 prompt tokens and only 32 uncached prompt tokens: 99.976270% cache-read share, not 12.7%. Derived cache-corrected service is 0.799523 s/request at c16 and 0.581830 at c32. The exact simulation gives 1.250746 versus 1.718714 req/s capacity and a 27.2278% c32 saturation-cost reduction. At the shared stable rate 1.125671 req/s, c16/c32 p95 completion is 30.9645/47.4594 s: c32 pays the allowed background pooling latency. At the strict 2.118591 req/s target, both are unstable; c32 remains 18.8747% short and its planning-all-in saturation cost is $0.00075721/request versus the $0.000614287 strict frontier. Queue drift over the last versus first trace tenth is +9,805.61 s for c32 and +29,397.96 s for c16. No failures/reloads are injected or observed. The source endpoint exposes 20.6956 GiB startup-free HBM but retained no decision-bearing power sample, recorded as an explicit observability boundary. Interpretation: c32 occupancy is the strongest stock scheduling interaction and materially beats c16, but scheduling alone does not produce an H200 economic candidate under the exact cache mix. Prefix reuse ranks second but the prior 5% bound requires at least 39.3708% exposed prefill wall share even at perfect hit realization; the only endpoint capture is contaminated by nearly complete reuse and cannot support that transfer. Chunked/async prefill ranks third and needs a matched exact-mix endpoint phase capture before promotion. CUDA-graph and hybrid local/global scheduling lack matched controls and rank behind them.

Next: close this bounded scheduling screen. Do not spend the retained H200 on c16/c32 replay. Reopen only with a new exact-mix endpoint capture that retains phase and power telemetry; the first matched arm should be stock c32 versus c32 chunked/async prefill, not another occupancy sweep.
