# Post-BF16-c32 successor selection

## Decision question

If the sole stock-BF16 c32 point is closed by the disjoint prefill/decode floors—or remains unresolved because the live attention program is scientifically invalid—what is the highest-information next intervention already supported by campaign evidence, and what exact result would justify one later paid arm?

This is `$0` portfolio evidence, not a provider-readiness wrapper. It must search across already-ranked project boundaries and return at most two materially different candidates, including one executable measurement candidate. It must not poll, edit, wait for, or infer from the live H200 owner.

## Scope

Read the campaign objective and only decision-relevant terminal artifacts from existing BF16, FP8/W4, MTP, runtime, hardware-price/capacity, full-stack-alternative, quality-route, and endpoint-failure projects. Distinguish scientific negatives from pre-science launcher failures. Preserve exact model/revision, workload, campaign quality requirement, latency-for-cost allowance, current market cost gate, and fresh shared production-price evidence. Do not browse or duplicate price research while the shared inventory is fresh.

Rank candidates by expected whole-request cost impact, quality risk, time to scope-matched evidence, staging/setup burden, provider availability, and whether existing actual-consumer or checkpoint artifacts make them executable. Explicitly examine at least:

- exact-revision online FP8 or another supported representation on H200/L40S/H100, separating measured consumer mechanisms from missing producer/quality/full-checkpoint evidence;
- the official Gemma assistant/MTP route against its existing acceptance and >=5% all-in gates, including whether prompt-prefill dominance makes decode savings irrelevant;
- a cheaper H100 or other current hardware route only where exact capacity, runtime, and price gates are content-bound, without transferring H200 performance;
- any model-change route that survived the exact weight-structure and tied-head screens.

Reject repeated controller/admission-only successors and any line whose next result requires another 60-GB checkpoint transfer before testing the mechanism. Prefer a checkpoint-free actual component or an exact already-resident/runtime-compatible arm whose result directly changes admission. For each retained candidate specify hypothesis, disjoint evidence, first scientific arm, comparator/quality boundary, stop rule, explicit prospective wall/provider/compute/storage/spend budget, and named trigger. Return one primary, at most one conditional backup, or typed none.

## Budgets and terminal

- Wall clock: 25 minutes.
- Compute: 1.5 local CPU-hours; no GPU.
- Storage: 384 MiB retained plus 128 MiB temporary.
- Spend: `$0`; provider query/mutation, checkpoint read/copy, image pull, compilation, and endpoint execution forbidden.

Write `SUCCESSOR_RANK.json`, concise `RESEARCH_LOG.md`, and terminal `STATUS.json`. Label all evidence as measured/derived/inferred and bind the terminal artifact hashes used.
