# Current exact-model mechanism and placement scan

## Question

What current, exact-GLM-4.7-Flash external evidence identifies the highest-value next cost mechanism or hardware/representation candidate, and where can that mechanism be measured safely versus deployed economically?

## Why it matters

The lab has spent multiple cycles inside inherited Nebius and V21 constraints. The RunPod A40 read-only preflight ended on unsupported REST paths and a GraphQL 403; that is provider control-plane evidence, not an A40 capacity or hardware-mechanism result. At the same time, current exact-model serving runtimes, quantizations, benchmark reports, and commercial placement rates may have changed. A short current scan can prevent another low-information allocation and keep experimental venue separate from production economics.

## Relevant evidence

- The completed hardware screen found an analytical A40 lead at a vendor-listed $0.44/hour: 192.022 output tok/s reaches the 5% gate and 203.704 reaches $0.60/M, but neither capacity nor performance was measured.
- Cloudflare Workers AI currently lists the exact model at $0.06/M input and $0.40/M output, or $0.88/M output-equivalent at the campaign's 8:1 ratio, but its public interface is not a matched quality replay.
- V21 is an irreproducible historical AWQ/vLLM/L40S prior, not a current control.

## Scope

Use one zero-cost researcher turn to search current official primary sources and reproducible first-party repositories for the exact GLM-4.7-Flash model. Cover only decision-relevant evidence: reproducible serving benchmarks or configs, current vLLM/SGLang or model-specific runtime support, maintained quantized checkpoints with derivation/quality evidence, hardware memory and bandwidth constraints, and currently listed on-demand/spot/reserved or owned-hardware economics. Bind retrieval time, exact URLs/revisions, content identities where practical, model/checkpoint/quantization/runtime/hardware/workload scope, and evidence labels.

Keep two columns conceptually separate for every candidate: (1) a safe available venue where the mechanism can be measured, and (2) a plausible production placement whose capacity, effective rate, interruption/recovery, storage, transfer, and orchestration costs would still need verification. A provider catalog or credential failure may close that provider path, but must not kill the underlying hardware or software mechanism. Conversely, a benchmark on convenient Nebius hardware does not establish production availability or economics.

Normalize any usable economics to the campaign's 8,192-input/1,024-output workload and dollars per million output-equivalent tokens. Treat mismatched model, representation, runtime, workload, concurrency, or quality evidence only as a prior. Calculate the break-even throughput for <=$0.6365/M and <=$0.60/M at each credible rate. Rank at most three candidates, nominate at most one immediate experiment or cheap falsifier that does not duplicate the active c8/c16/c32 project, and state its exact admission and stop rules. If no candidate has a credible path to >=5% impact, record that negative result.

Do not use credentials, mutate a provider, reserve capacity, run a benchmark, edit another project's runner, repair V21, or create a generic provider catalog. This project has $0 budget and no provider ownership.

## Available resources

Use official model/runtime/checkpoint repositories and documentation, official hardware specifications, current provider catalogs and pricing, the shared evidence tools, `campaign_100`, and the completed comparator/hardware-screen artifacts.

## Portfolio-changing result

A scope-labeled candidate with a concrete measurement venue, production-placement hypothesis, and conservative >=5% path earns one later bounded experiment. Provider access failure redirects venue search rather than terminating the mechanism. No credible candidate closes the external scan as a useful negative result and keeps capacity on the matched runnable-control project.
