# Major-provider comparator

## Question

What current major-provider GLM-4.7-Flash offering is the cheapest credible comparator for the campaign's fixed background-agent workload, and what matched price, quality, reliability, and completion threshold must the local stack beat?

## Why it matters

The campaign objective is comparative, but the retained provider evidence is only a vendor-price anchor. Cloudflare's July 13 listing implied $0.88 per million output-equivalent tokens at the campaign's 8:1 input:output ratio; Fireworks and Baseten listed a different GLM 4.7 model. V21's derived $0.670/M is directionally below that anchor but has no matched provider workload, quality, availability, or service evidence, so no competitive claim is permitted.

## Relevant evidence

- `campaign_100/analysis/PROVIDER_ANCHORS_2026-07-13.md`.
- `campaign_100/DECISION_CONTINUE_NARROWLY_2026-07-14.json` and its unmatched-comparator claim limit.
- The fixed 8,192-input/1,024-output workload, prospective quality scorer, and local V21 cost/service artifacts.
- Official current model, pricing, and service documentation from major inference providers.

## Scope

Run one zero-cost, source-backed comparator audit using current official primary sources. Bind retrieval time and page/content identities. Verify exact model identity, context support, input/output/cached-token prices, endpoint availability, request controls, rate/concurrency limits, reliability terms, and whether a matched 8,192/1,024 replay with retained raw responses is technically possible. Normalize prices to dollars per million output-equivalent tokens at 8:1 and state caching and batch assumptions. Distinguish vendor-reported price/capability from measured evidence.

Do not access a paid account, use credentials, send model requests, incur spend, weaken the local quality/background envelope, or treat GLM 4.7 as GLM-4.7-Flash. If no runnable exact-model endpoint is publicly documented, record that falsifier and the minimal authority needed for a later matched replay rather than drafting another broad plan.

## Available resources

Use the retained provider anchors, campaign workload and scorer contracts, shared evidence tools, and official provider documentation. Budget is $0 and the result should fit one researcher turn. The provisional threshold is Cloudflare's vendor-listed $0.88/M output-equivalent at 8:1; refresh or replace it from current primary evidence.

## Portfolio-changing result

A current exact-model offering with a runnable matched endpoint becomes the named comparator and supplies the precise threshold for a later paired replay. A cheaper credible anchor raises the local economic gate immediately. If no major provider supports a matched exact-model comparison, retain the strongest current vendor anchor, label the campaign unmatched, and identify the smallest external authorization needed to close it.
